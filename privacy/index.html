Privacy Policy

1. Controller

The data controller for Autoclipz1 is Marco Lucia, reachable at marcolucia247@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

  • TikTok OAuth tokens — stored locally on your device to maintain your TikTok connection. Never sent to our servers.
  • Video files — processed locally on your device for subtitle generation and formatting. Not uploaded to any third-party server beyond TikTok at your explicit request.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and publish videos on your behalf when you explicitly approve.
  • To generate and apply subtitles to your videos locally.
  • To diagnose errors and improve the App.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law or to fulfil the core service (i.e., transmitting your approved video to TikTok).

4. TikTok Data

When you connect your TikTok account, TikTok’s own Privacy Policy also applies. We only request the minimum permissions required to post videos: video.publish and, where applicable, video.upload.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained locally until you disconnect your TikTok account or uninstall the App. Processed video files are deleted from the App’s working directory after successful publication or upon your request.

8. Security
